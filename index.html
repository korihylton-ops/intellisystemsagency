FAQ

Questions We Actually Get Asked

What does an AI automation partner actually do for a trade business?
We look at the manual work already happening in your business — quoting, scheduling, follow-up, reporting — and build automation and software that does it for you, without changing how your team actually works day to day.
When should I start automating instead of just hiring more office staff?
If the same admin task is repeated every week — the same follow-up message, the same quote format, the same manual data entry — it's usually cheaper and faster to automate it than to hire and train someone to keep doing it by hand.
Do you work with smaller, independent trade businesses, or only larger companies?
Both. The Diagnostic and Pilot steps exist specifically so a smaller business can start with one automated process before committing to a larger build.
Can you guarantee more leads or higher Google rankings?
No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Nobody controls Google's or an AI model's algorithm. What we can guarantee is the work itself — a proper technical setup, content built to be found and cited, and clear reporting on what's actually changing.
What's the difference between SEO and GEO?
SEO is about ranking in a traditional Google search. GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — is about making sure AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude actually understand your business well enough to mention and recommend it. Both matter now, and they overlap more than people expect.
Which tools and platforms can you connect or build on?
We build on mainstream, well-supported platforms and can typically connect to whatever CRM, accounting or scheduling software you're already using, rather than forcing a switch on day one.
Do I own the software once it's built?
Yes. Full builds are handed over with the source code and account access. You're not locked into paying us indefinitely just to keep using what you paid for.
